## About Me

I am **Tuan-Binh Tran** (Vietnamese: **Trần Tuấn Bình**; also written as **Tran Tuan Binh**), a master’s student at VinUniversity supervised by [Dr. Huynh Thanh Trung](https://thanhtrunghuynh93.github.io/) and [Dr. Tung Kieu](https://tungk.github.io/pages/publication/). Previously, I interned with the FPT AI Residency program at FPT Software under Dr. Tung’s supervision and worked as a research assistant at VNU University of Engineering and Technology (VNU-UET) with [Dr. Duc-Trong Le](https://uet.vnu.edu.vn/~trongld/).

I am exploring a range of research topics as I develop my long-term research focus. My current interests include:

- **Data Mining**: Time series analysis and recommender systems.
- **Machine Learning**: Generative models, particularly diffusion models, and continual learning.

Outside of research, I enjoy playing badminton 🏸, working out at the gym 💪🏻, and singing 🎤.
